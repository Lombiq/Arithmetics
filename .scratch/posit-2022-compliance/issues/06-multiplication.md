## Comments

- **Pre-implementation contract (Posit Standard (2022), original PDF, sections 3.1-3.3, pp. 6-7; 4.1, p. 8; 5.1 and 5.4, p. 9):** `multiplication(posit1, posit2)` (5.4) returns `posit1 × posit2, rounded` (4.1: "Operation results are regarded as exact prior to rounding"). Both inputs and the result have the same E2 precision `n` (8, 16 or 32; 5.1 requires equal precisions and mixed inputs use the 6.1 conversions first). Each finite posit is the exact signed dyadic value of its 3.3 fields (an integer multiple of minPos = 2^(-4n+8), at most maxPos = 2^(4n-8), 3.2), so the product is an exact finite dyadic value whose complete significand product is kept until the single rounding. Any NaR input gives NaR (5.1) and that test precedes every zero shortcut, so `0 × NaR` and `NaR × 0` are NaR; otherwise a zero operand gives the exactly expressible `0` unchanged. The product sign is the XOR of the operand signs. Rounding is the literal 4.1 algorithm: exactly expressible values are returned unchanged; `|x| > maxPos` returns `sign(x) × maxPos`; `0 < |x| < minPos` returns `sign(x) × minPos` (finite nonzero operands can never produce NaR or `0`); otherwise take the adjacent `n`-bit posits `u < x < w`, let `U` be the `n`-bit representation of `u` and `v` the `(n+1)`-bit posit with representation `U1`, and return `u` when `x < v` or when `x = v` and the LSB of `U` is 0, else `w`. No arithmetic midpoint, truncation or round-half-up is assumed, and neither legacy truncating Posit32/Posit32E2 product is an oracle.
- **Interpretations and boundaries:** Only E2 8/16/32 and the Posit32 compatibility surface follow this contract; non-E2 (E0/E1/E3/E4) products keep their legacy algorithm and regression coverage, with any justified correction documented. Section 4.1 for a negative `x` is the negation of 4.1 for `|x|` (two's complement negation maps adjacent posits to adjacent posits and `U1` boundaries to `U1` boundaries, and flips the LSB parity of `U` consistently with swapping `u` and `w`), so the product may round the magnitude and negate the encoding; the test oracle still applies 4.1 directly to the signed exact product. The `Posit * int` overloads keep their existing integer constructor conversion and then use the standard product; integer-conversion semantics belong to the integer-conversion tickets. `MultiplyIntoQuire`, fused products (4.2, tickets 10 and 11), division and addition are outside this ticket.
- **Independent expected values (planned):** The exact product is formed from the ticket 02 section 3.3 signed-field `BigInteger` decoder as `(c1·c2, e1+e2)` and rounded by the shared literal 4.1 oracle owned by ticket 05 (`PositRoundingReference.Round(PositEncodingReference, size)`), never by production arithmetic, IEEE casts or midpoint assumptions. Evidence: all 65,536 posit8 pairs; hand-checked ties, sticky bits, regime/exponent transitions, exact powers, maxPos/minPos edges, finite overflow and nonzero underflow in both signs; hard and seeded posit16/32 vectors; NaR/zero/sign combinations; Posit32 versus Posit32E2 agreement; retained non-E2 regressions.
